
# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 11, 2026**

Smart Contact Manager ("the application") is a student project that helps users manage their accounts and save contact information.

## Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the application may process:

- Your name and email address.
- Account and login information.
- Contact details you choose to save, such as names, phone numbers and email addresses.
- Profile pictures and contact images you upload.
- Information required to verify your email address and secure your account.

## How We Use Information

We use this information to:
- Create and manage user accounts.
- Provide contact management features.
- Verify email addresses.
- Authenticate users.
- Store uploaded images.
- Maintain application security and functionality.

## Third-Party Services

The application may use the following services:

- Render for application hosting.
- TiDB Cloud for database storage.
- Cloudinary for image storage.
- Google and GitHub for login authentication.
- Google Gmail services for sending verification emails.

These services may process information as necessary to provide their functionality.

## Information Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers mentioned above or disclosed when required by law.

## Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect user information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

## Children's Privacy

This application is not intended for children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as the application develops. The latest version will be available at this URL.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the project maintainer using the support email displayed on the application's Google consent screen.
